Privacy policy and terms

Personal Assistant · last updated 17 November 2026

Who uses it

Personal Assistant is used only by its owner, Samuel Tait, with his own Google account. Nobody else can sign in to it.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission it reads his Gmail messages and Google Calendar events, read-only. A separate permission lets it send one summary email a day, to the owner only. A third permission lets it add events to the owner's calendar and answer invitations he has received, only when he approves each one from the brief. Added events are marked [PA] and invite nobody; answering an invitation lets its organiser know, as any reply would. It does not delete, change or forward mail, does not change or delete existing calendar events, and never sends mail to anyone but the owner.

How the data is handled

Data is processed and stored only on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. To write the daily summary, the relevant message text is processed by Anthropic's Claude through the owner's own Anthropic account, under that account's terms and privacy settings.

Google user data is used only to produce the owner's own summary, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections. Stored data is deleted by removing the tool's folder from the owner's computer.
