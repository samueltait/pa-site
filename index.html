Personal Assistant

A private tool by Samuel Tait

Personal Assistant is a private, single-user tool. Each weekday morning it reads its owner's own Gmail and Google Calendar, read-only, and emails him a short summary: bills and due dates, messages that need a reply, upcoming appointments and newsletters.

When he approves an item in the summary, it can also add it to his calendar as a tentative hold, or answer a calendar invitation for him.

It is not offered to anyone else. There are no other users, no accounts to sign up for and no public service.
